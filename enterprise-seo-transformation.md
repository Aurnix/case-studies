# Enterprise SEO Transformation Case Study

## Large Texas Community College System
*12-Month Engagement | $2.5-5M Revenue Impact | 100,000+ Pages*

---

### 📊 The Challenge

- **90,000+ students** across 6 campuses with 5,000+ staff members
- **100,000+ page website** with millions of monthly visits
- **Multiple legacy CMS systems** including homegrown platforms from early 2000s
- **Limited budget** requiring surgical precision over broad optimization

### 🎯 Strategic Approach

Rather than attempting to optimize 100,000+ pages, I developed an impact analysis framework that identified the critical 1% of pages driving 80% of enrollment value.

**Focus Areas:**
- Program-specific landing pages with direct enrollment pathways
- High-intent search term targets aligned with student decision journey
- Technical infrastructure improvements affecting site-wide performance

### 📈 Results

#### Traffic & Visibility
- **20%** YoY increase in overall organic traffic
- **30%+** growth to program-specific pages (highest conversion intent)
- **94%** increase in keywords ranking in positions 1-3
- **Top 3** SERP dominance for critical enrollment terms

#### Business Impact
- **$2.5-5M** in annual tuition revenue generated
- **10,400-20,800%** ROI on SEO investment
- **~1,800-3,600** additional student enrollments attributed to organic
- **$21.6-72M** in projected 4-year student lifetime value

### 🚀 Key Innovations

- Developed repeatable framework for identifying high-impact optimization opportunities at scale
- Integrated cross-functional workflows across Wrike, Monday.com, and Salesforce
- Unified SEO strategy across disparate CMS platforms without infrastructure overhaul
- Created sustainable processes enabling in-house team continuity

## Methodology: Strategic Precision

### The Framework

1. **📊 Data-Driven Target Identification**  
   Finding pages and keywords that actually drive revenue

2. **🎯 Focused Optimization**  
   Concentrating on the critical 1-5% that delivers 80% of value

3. **⚙️ Scalable Frameworks**  
   Building repeatable processes for 1,000 or 1 million pages

4. **📈 Measurable Results**  
   Clear attribution between SEO investment and revenue

### Why This Approach Works

- ✅ No wasted resources on low-impact optimizations
- ✅ Faster time to measurable results (3-6 months)
- ✅ Clear revenue attribution
- ✅ Sustainable processes teams can maintain
